Resident Guide

Get ready for move-in day in Singapore, learn how to navigate our co-working spaces and community events, and review building guidelines.

Move-in checklist

  • Confirm your check-in time and key-card access details with our team.
  • Keep your booking confirmation and photo ID ready for arrival.
  • Review your package inclusions (such as utilities and cleaning schedules) before packing.
  • Pack personal toiletries, work equipment, devices, and any specific items you need.
  • Inspect your room on arrival and report any existing maintenance issues promptly.

Using shared spaces & community guidelines

  • Keep study pods and co-working areas quiet and take video calls in designated zones.
  • Clean up after yourself in communal kitchens and dining areas.
  • Respect building-wide 24/7 security and keep emergency corridors clear.
  • Participate respectfully in weekly community events and keep shared lounge spaces tidy.

Reporting a maintenance issue

  1. Note your room number or the specific building area requiring attention.
  2. Describe the problem clearly and when you first noticed it.
  3. Submit your request using the resident support portal or contact details provided at check-in.
